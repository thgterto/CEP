## 2025-04-17 - Array manipulation bottlenecks in SPC module
**Learning:** Replaced `map`, `slice`, and `push`/`shift` with pre-allocated arrays and single-pass explicit `for` loops in statistical functions (`computeXbarR`, `computeXbarS`, `computeCUSUM`, `computeEWMA`). This drastically avoids V8 closure overhead, GC pauses from array reallocations, and spread operator stack overflows. In tight statistical computations, pre-allocated `new Array(size)` is vastly superior. `Float64Array.sort()` is also ~5-15x faster than native `Array.prototype.sort((a,b)=>a-b)`.
**Action:** Always favor single-pass `for` loops with pre-allocated arrays (`new Array()`) and `Float64Array` sorting over functional array methods for hot-path statistical or mathematical functions on large datasets.
